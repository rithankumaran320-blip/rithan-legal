Privacy Policy

Rithan & Friends · Effective and last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rithan & Friends ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Rithan & Friends mobile application ("the App"). The App serves three types of accounts: User (shopping and orders), Player (games, offer cards and rewards) and Business (sellers who list and sell products). By creating an account or using the App you agree to this policy.

1.2 Information from Google Sign-In

If you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Sign up with Google", Google shares with us your email address and name (and a unique account identifier). We do not receive your Google password. We use this information only to create and sign in to your account. We still ask you for a mobile number to complete registration.

1.3 Information created when you use the App

  • Orders and payments: items, quantities, prices, offer cards applied, order and delivery status, payment status and return or refund requests. Online payments are processed by Cashfree; we receive payment status but do not store your card, UPI or bank credentials.
  • Game activity: game progress, scores, offer cards, rewards and winner records linked to your Player account.
  • Notifications: a device push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send order and game notifications.
  • Location: if you grant permission, we collect your device's current location while you use the App for delivery and nearby-store features. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Technical data: basic logs and error information needed to keep the App secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To process, deliver, return and refund orders, and to settle payments with Businesses.
  • To run games, issue and validate offer cards and rewards.
  • To send order updates, game updates and important account messages.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, duplicate accounts, cheating and misuse, including blocking accounts that break our Terms.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal effect on you, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertisements

The App may display video and banner advertisements that are added and managed by us. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

5. Data retention

  • We keep your account data while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes (for example order and payment records).

6. Account and data deletion

To delete your account and data: email rithankumaran320@gmail.com from your registered email address (or mention your registered mobile number) with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and complete deletion as described above.

Deleting your account removes your profile, Player progress and linked data. It may not be possible to undo this.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may:

  • access and correct your personal information (you can edit most details inside the App);
  • request deletion of your account and data (section 6);
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it (this may stop some features from working);
  • turn off notifications in your phone settings;
  • make a complaint to us (section 11).

8. Security

We protect your data using access controls, row-level security in our database, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and trusted providers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Users under 18 should use the App with a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes we will inform you in the App or by other reasonable means. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy.
